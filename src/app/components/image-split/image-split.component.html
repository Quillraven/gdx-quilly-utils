Spritesheet Splitter

Split a spritesheet into individual tiles, either by a number of rows and columns or by the size of a single tile. Upload your spritesheet, adjust the settings and click Split Image.

More details
  • Split by num tiles: divides the sheet into a grid of the given rows and columns.
  • Split by size: uses the size of one tile (e.g. 32x32); partial tiles at the edges are ignored.
  • Optimize frames: applies the Sheet Optimizer logic first, trimming transparent borders and aligning all frames to a common bounding box.
