Spritesheet Splitter

Split a spritesheet into individual image tiles. This tool supports two split modes:

  • Split by num tiles: Divides your spritesheet into a grid based on the number of rows and columns you specify.
  • Split by size: Divides your spritesheet based on the dimensions of a single tile (e.g. 32x32 pixels).

Upload your spritesheet first, then configure the settings and click on Split Image to generate the individual tiles. When splitting by size, any partial tiles at the edges are ignored.
